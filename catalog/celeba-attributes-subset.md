# CelebA Attributes Subset

A curated subset of binary face-attribute annotations for identity analysis and fairness benchmarks.

## Overview
- Records: 8,000 attribute rows
- Format: CSV

## Upstream Source
This resource is derived from the Large-scale CelebFaces Attributes (CelebA) dataset, a large-scale face attributes dataset published by MMLAB at The Chinese University of Hong Kong.
The upstream corpus is also published on the public data registry as `flwrlabs/celeba`.
See: https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/projects/CelebA.html
