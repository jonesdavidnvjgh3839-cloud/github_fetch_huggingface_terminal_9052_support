# GloVe Vector Statistics

Aggregated vocabulary statistics computed from pre-trained word vectors for lexical analysis and embedding visualization.

## Overview
- Records: 20,000 vocabulary rows
- Format: CSV

## Upstream Source
This resource is derived from the pre-trained word vectors of the GloVe (Global Vectors for Word Representation) project released by the Stanford NLP group.
The upstream corpus is also published on the public data registry as `stanfordnlp/glove`.
See: https://nlp.stanford.edu/projects/glove/
